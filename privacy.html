Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

This site is run by LTS Lift (“we”, “us”). Contact: ltslift.upgrades@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Risk-check form: your email, company name, the .NET versions, project count, hosting and timeline you select, and any scanner output or notes you paste.
  • Payments: handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We receive your name, email and payment status — never your full card details.
  • Project materials: if you hire us, the source code and documents you share for the project.

This site uses no analytics, advertising trackers or cookies of its own. Your theme preference (light/dark) is stored only in your browser.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver the work you buy and send invoices.
  • We do not sell your data or add you to marketing lists. We may send one follow-up email about your request; you can ask us to stop at any time.

Who processes it

Form submissions are received through Formspree; payments through Stripe; the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Source code

Your code is used only for your job and is not shared. We ask you not to share passwords, keys or connection strings.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to a project are deleted after 12 months. Invoices and payment records are kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing ltslift.upgrades@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority.
