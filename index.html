What "end of support" means for your business

Nothing breaks overnight. That's what makes it easy to ignore.

No more security fixes

After 10 November 2026, Microsoft stops patching .NET 8 and .NET 9. New vulnerabilities found in the runtime stay open in your app.

Azure Functions has its own deadline

The Azure Functions in-process model also reaches end of support on 10 November 2026. Those apps must move to the isolated worker model before they can run on .NET 10.

Audits and customers will ask

Security questionnaires, pen tests and compliance reviews routinely flag unsupported runtimes. "We're on an unsupported version" is a hard answer to give a customer.

.NET 10 is the current Long Term Support release, supported until November 2028. Sources: Microsoft .NET Blog, .NET support policy.

Frequently asked questions

Will my app stop working on 10 November?

No. It keeps running. What stops is security patching: vulnerabilities discovered after that date won't be fixed for .NET 8 or .NET 9. The risk grows over time rather than all at once.

How do you get access to our code?

The simplest way is read access to your Git repository (GitHub, Azure DevOps, GitLab, Bitbucket) as a collaborator, which you revoke after delivery. A zip of the source works too. We never need access to your production servers, databases or cloud accounts.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. We're happy to sign your NDA before seeing any code, or provide a simple mutual NDA.

What about secrets and security?

We don't need your passwords, connection strings or API keys — please remove them from anything you share, or point us to placeholder values. Your code is used only for your job and is not shared.

Who deploys the upgrade?

You do (or your hosting provider / IT partner). We deliver a pull request plus a deployment checklist and rollback plan, so whoever deploys knows exactly what changed. We're available to answer questions while you deploy.

We don't have any tests. Is that a problem?

No, but it changes what "verified" means. Without tests we confirm the build passes and the app starts and responds locally, and we tell you exactly what we couldn't verify. Adding tests is available as a separate quote.

What if it turns out bigger than the package?

We tell you before doing the extra work, with a fixed price for it. You can accept, or stop and keep everything delivered so far. No surprise invoices.

How do I pay?

The audit and every upgrade are paid in full before work starts (upgrades after you accept the fixed quote), and are covered by the fix-it-or-refund guarantee. Prices are in USD.

Why .NET 10 and not .NET 9?

.NET 9 loses support on the same day as .NET 8. .NET 10 is the Long Term Support release, supported until November 2028.
